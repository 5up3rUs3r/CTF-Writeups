## Event Info

> NahamCon CTF is the annual Capture the Flag competition accompanying the NahamCon security conference, organized by JohnHammond. The CTF runs for 48 hours and covers a wide range of categories including web exploitation, cryptography, steganography, forensics, binary exploitation, OSINT, scripting, mobile, and miscellaneous challenges — making it approachable for all skill levels while still including expert-tier challenges.  
> **CTFtime:** https://ctftime.org/event/2782  
> **Official Platform:** https://ctf.nahamcon.com

## About

NahamCon CTF 2025 was a 48-hour jeopardy-style competition held alongside the NahamCon virtual security conference. With 1709 teams competing across a diverse challenge set, the event was a great opportunity to practice across multiple cybersecurity domains — from low-level binary exploitation to web attacks, cryptography, and OSINT.
